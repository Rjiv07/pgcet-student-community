# Sprint 006 — College knowledge base

## Goal
Create a useful initial college directory for PGCET aspirants and current MCA students. Start with ten institution profiles, then expand based on student feedback.

## College profile structure
Each directory card opens a dedicated route. The college name and location appear at the top, followed by three consistent programme columns: MCA, MBA, and M.Tech.

At this stage, every programme is marked **Verification pending**. A programme card is a place to collect information, not a claim that the institution currently offers that programme. Only change its status after confirming the current offering from an official institution or authoritative admissions source.

## Data to collect per programme
- Official programme page and current brochure
- Admission route, eligibility, and applicable academic year
- Fees with academic year and official source
- Curriculum, specialisations, and facilities
- Programme-specific placement data with reporting year and methodology
- Student experiences, labelled as first-hand and dated, with permission
- Last-checked date and reviewer

## Editorial rules
- Do not call the shortlist an official “top 10” or imply a ranking.
- Do not invent fee figures, eligibility, programme availability, placements, facilities, or student testimonials.
- Distinguish institution-wide data from course-specific outcomes.
- Mark unknown information as “Not yet documented” and show sources and checked dates as information is verified.
- Student feedback should inform priorities and corrections, but not be treated as proof of official facts.

## Expansion
Invite students to suggest corrections or sources through the contribution forms. Add more institutions after the initial ten have useful coverage or when student feedback demonstrates a clear need.
